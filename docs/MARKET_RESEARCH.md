# Spott market research

**Checked:** 2026-11-17. Pricing is publicly listed USD/region-specific pricing where available. Competitor reviews are self-selected snapshots, not proof that every organizer has the same experience. User demand and willingness to pay for Spott remain unvalidated.

## Target user and problem

Individual community and professional event organizers who need to discover ideas, draft an event page, collect RSVPs, check attendees in, and understand whether the event filled. Spott already combines event discovery, AI draft generation, registration, organizer management, QR check-in and exports. Paid ticket checkout is not part of the current product; displayed prices are paid offline to organizers.

## Positioning hypothesis

**Spott helps independent organizers go from an event idea to a discoverable, RSVP-ready event with AI-assisted drafting and simple check-in, while keeping ticket payment outside the platform until payment and refund operations are ready.** This combines discovery and organizer workflow, but has not yet been validated against paying users.

## Buyer validation

Interview 5–8 local organizers: what they repeat for every event, where RSVPs are lost, how they handle check-in, what they pay today, and which tool they would stop using. Seek one bounded paid pilot before selecting a price or turning on a subscription.

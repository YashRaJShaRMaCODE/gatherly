# Spott

## Event discovery and organizer workflow

Spott helps people discover events and gives individual organizers a workflow for drafting, publishing, registering guests and checking them in.

### Who it may help

Community and professional event organizers who repeat the same planning and attendee tasks across small events.

### What works in the code

- Public event discovery and detail pages
- AI-assisted event draft generation with bounded daily quota
- Organizer event management, registration, cancellation and QR check-in
- Registration CSV export
- Individual Clerk sign-in and Convex-backed data

### Current boundaries

The Pro subscription must be configured by the owner in Clerk. Ticket prices are informational; attendees pay organizers outside Spott. No team subscription, ticket checkout, payout, refund flow, real customer demand or revenue is verified. Production backup/restore and privacy review remain open.

### Revenue hypothesis

An individual organizer subscription may fit repeat hosts who save time across events. First validate this with 5–8 interviews and one bounded pilot before setting a price or switching on live billing.
